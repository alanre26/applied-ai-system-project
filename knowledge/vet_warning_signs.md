PawPal can help plan care, but it cannot tell you what is wrong with a sick pet. Call your vet or an emergency clinic for any of these:

- Trouble breathing, collapse, or seizures.
- Eating something toxic, such as chocolate, grapes, xylitol, lilies (for cats), or human medicine.
- Vomiting or diarrhea that keeps happening or has blood in it.
- Not eating for more than a day.
- Straining to pee, especially in male cats.

Most pets should also have a checkup at least once a year, and older pets often twice a year.
