title: Giving medication
species: all

min_priority: high
tip: Give meds at the same time every day and never skip or double a dose.
---
Medication should be treated as a must-do task. Missing a dose can make some treatments stop working, and for things like insulin or seizure meds it can be dangerous.

- Give each dose at the same time every day so it is easy to remember.
- If a dose is missed, do not give two doses at once to make up for it. Call your vet and ask what to do.
- Finish the full course of antibiotics, even if your pet seems better.
- Monthly preventatives (flea, tick, heartworm) are easy to forget. Set a reminder for the same day each month.
- Never give human medicine to a pet unless a vet tells you to. Some common ones, like ibuprofen and acetaminophen, are toxic to dogs and cats.
