Play keeps pets mentally active and builds a bond with the owner.

- Cats usually do well with a couple of short play sessions a day, around 10 to 15 minutes each.
- Puzzle feeders and short training sessions are good ways to tire a dog out mentally.
- If time is tight, a few minutes of play still helps.
