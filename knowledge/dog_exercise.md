Daily exercise keeps dogs healthy and cuts down on chewing, barking, and other problem behavior.

- Most adult dogs need at least 30 minutes to an hour of activity a day. Young dogs and working breeds often need more.
- Split it into two or more walks if you can, such as morning and evening.
- On hot days, walk early or late. If the pavement is too hot to hold your hand on for a few seconds, it is too hot for paws.
- Older dogs still need walks, just shorter and gentler ones.
