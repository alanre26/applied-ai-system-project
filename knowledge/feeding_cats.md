---
Cats like routine, and steady meals help keep their weight in a healthy range.

- Feed adult cats at least two meals a day. Smaller, more frequent meals work well for many cats.
- Keep feeding times consistent.
- A cat that stops eating for more than a day should see a vet. Cats can get seriously sick from not eating, even for a short time.
- Do not give cats dog food. It does not have everything a cat needs.
