A clean litter box keeps cats using it and helps you notice health problems early.

- Scoop at least once a day.
- Fully empty, wash, and refill the box about once a week.
- A common rule is one box per cat, plus one extra.
- If your cat is going a lot more, a lot less, or straining, call a vet. A male cat straining with little or no urine is an emergency.
