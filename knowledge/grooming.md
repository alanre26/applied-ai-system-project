Grooming keeps the coat healthy and gives you a chance to check for lumps, ticks, or sore spots.

- Short-haired pets can usually be brushed once or twice a week. Long-haired pets do better with daily brushing.
- Trim nails about every 3 to 4 weeks, or when you hear them clicking on the floor.
- Most dogs only need a bath every month or two unless they get dirty. Most cats rarely need baths.
- Grooming can usually move to another day if time is short.
