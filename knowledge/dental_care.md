Dental disease is very common in adult dogs and cats.

- Brush your pet's teeth daily if you can, or at least a few times a week.
- Only use toothpaste made for pets. Human toothpaste can be harmful if swallowed.
- Bad breath, red gums, or trouble chewing are signs to see a vet.
