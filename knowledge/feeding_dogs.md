Regular meals keep a dog's energy steady and make house training easier.

- Most adult dogs eat two meals a day, about 8 to 12 hours apart. Puppies usually need three or four smaller meals.
- Feed at roughly the same times every day.
- Measure food instead of guessing. The amount on the bag is a starting point, and your vet can tell you if your dog needs more or less.
- Treats should be a small part of the day's food, around 10 percent.
- Large, deep-chested dogs can be at risk of bloat. Avoid hard exercise right before and after big meals.
