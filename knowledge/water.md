---
title: Fresh water
species: all
keywords: water, bowl, hydration, drink, fountain
min_priority: high
tip: Refill water with fresh water every day and wash the bowl often.
---
Pets should always have clean water available.

- Dump and refill the bowl with fresh water at least once a day.
- Wash the bowl every few days so slime does not build up.
- Bring water on long walks and hot days.
- A big change in how much your pet drinks, more or less, is worth a call to the vet.
